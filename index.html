About

I’m Senior Engineering Manager for robotics at Noah Medical. I joined as employee #1 in 2019 and have worked on the Galaxy bronchoscopy robot since the earliest prototype — through FDA clearance, and into clinical use in hospitals.

My background is in motion control and robot software. I did my Ph.D. at Vanderbilt on magnetic endoscopic robots and have built control algorithms and software for continuum and magnetic robots as well as robot arms.
